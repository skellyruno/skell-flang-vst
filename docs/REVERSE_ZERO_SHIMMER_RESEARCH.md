# Reverse zero-shimmer research — no production change

Chorus and Flang are protected: no Source file, parameter ID, preset or engine changes. Investigated avoiding the two interpolated octave reads when the Reverse shimmer contribution is zero. The local standalone fixture reproduces the parent Reverse routine and its bounded Delay operations in isolation; the full current FlangDSP header was not available locally (direct fetch DNS failed, older checkpoint differs). This is not actual integrated engine or DAW timing.

A 36-input timing attempt timed out; no incomplete results counted. Three selected private inputs (two dry, one processed), three settings (zero shimmer, .5 shimmer, alternating), three paired runs each. Both candidates 9/9 exact and finite. The inner-loop runtime branch had median paired savings +28.33%, -10.86%, +8.33%; specializing the read decision outside the grain loop gave +34.06%, -4.18%, +16.03%. Negative saving means slower. Timing is native wall time including prepare, not FL Studio CPU. Keep both experimental because active-shimmer cost still regressed in the small sample and full integration is unverified. Only specialized fixture and benchmark committed. No private audio, filenames or individual metrics in Git.
